Rotate Muse 0.1.0 beta

The beta is installed as its own app. Open Rotate Muse from Cocoon's app drawer.
Cocoon remains your default launcher.

1. Choose Themes, select Neon Nostalgia or Celestial Dream, then Preview.
2. Or choose My Videos > Import video or GIF and select your own local media.
   Import creates a private copy. Your original is preserved.
3. Use Clock to change format, date, font, opacity, color or position.
   In display mode you can drag the clock. Back or Exit leaves the display.
4. Settings controls framing, dimming and the display timeout.
   Current tested device setting: dim after 30 seconds; end display after
   two minutes. Android's own screen timeout applies afterward.

This is a manual first beta. Automatic swivel activation is not enabled.
The included themes are procedural demonstrations, not cinematic videos.
Test MP4/GIF fixtures were generated from the app and remain in this folder.

The APK is a private debug test build. Install only if needed; the app was
already installed during testing. Source and Windows build/install scripts
are in the source ZIP. See README.md and VALIDATION.md for limitations.
No account, network, overlay, accessibility or broad storage permission needed.
