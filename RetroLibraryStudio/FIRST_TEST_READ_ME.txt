v1.5.6 FIRST TEST: Virtual 512 GB Card Bar

1. Extract into a new app folder and run Start_Retro_Library_Studio.bat. Keep your existing NAS workspace.
2. With My Games open, find the 512 GB capacity strip above the game tiles. Click Exact sizes… for the precise staged byte count, AI budget and reserve.
3. Refresh, add a small test game, or complete a CHD conversion. Confirm the staged total changes and no 512 GB file is created.
4. Once your real 512 GB microSD arrives, choose Copy to card, choose the physical drive root, then run Check what would be copied.
5. Confirm REAL CARD shows the measured formatted capacity, occupied bytes, new transfer bytes and free space after transfer. Do not copy unless there are no conflicts.

The read-only preview does not copy, delete, or format anything.
